Everything on whatmodel.sh is open data. Pick how you'd like to help.

## Benchmark your own machine

Measure how fast the models you already have run on your hardware, then share the results if you like.

[Run the benchmark →](/bench)

## Add a benchmark result from a source

Found a score we don't have? Tell us the model, the score and where it's published. We accept:

- Open leaderboard datasets, like LMArena, Epoch AI and the Open ASR Leaderboard
- Official model cards from the model's makers
- Research papers under an open licence (CC BY, CC BY-SA or CC0)

Aggregator sites such as Artificial Analysis or OpenRouter can't be used. Their terms don't allow their data to be copied and republished openly, and everything here has to be free for anyone to share.

[Add a result →](https://github.com/jakemanger/whatmodel/issues/new?template=paper-results.yml)
