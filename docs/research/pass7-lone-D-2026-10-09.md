# Pass 7, batch D: comparators for stranded speech-to-text results (2026-10-09)

Candidates are in `pass7-lone-D-2026-10-09.json`. `data/` was not edited.

Batch D covered 12 openly licensed STT sources with 69 lone rows: the five NVIDIA Parakeet/Canary cards, the
Distil-Whisper large-v3.5 card, the NVIDIA DLE Jasper and QuartzNet READMEs, IBM Granite Speech 4.1 and 5.0,
raven.eval and the SpeechBrain cards. For each lone row I opened the pinned revision, found the table the value
came from, and checked whether the other columns are the authors' own measurements under the same setup.

## Result

- **17 rows proposed, all `by: "lab"`, none independent.** They all come from the distil-large-v3.5 card. They are
  the Distil-Whisper team's own earlier models in the same two tables:
  - `distil-v3` (short-form table): 7 sets.
  - `distil-v2` and `distil-v3` (long-form table, sequential decoding): 5 sets each.
- **No new models, benchmarks or ledger ids.** Every row reuses `card2-distil-whisper-distil-large-v3-5` and its
  exact pinned README URL.
- **12 lone runs now have at least 2 models.** All are from the distil card: 7 short-form runs have 2 models and 5
  long-form runs have 3.
- **Effect on scoring.** I imported the 17 rows into a scratch copy of `data/` and ran `lib/score.ts` `toCells` on
  it. 10 of the 12 runs count. 25 more results count than before (12,299 to 12,324): the 10 stranded distil-v3.5
  rows and 15 of the new rows.
- **`ami-wer` and `earnings22-wer` still don't count.** `toCells` also needs 3 models per test across all counting
  runs (`MIN_MODELS = 3`), and these two tests have only 2. Either of the first two owner decisions below would
  fix that.
- **57 lone rows stay lone:** the 5 NVIDIA cards (49), DLE (4), Granite 4.1 and 5.0 (2), raven (1) and SpeechBrain
  (1).
