  Seed2.0 Pro and Lite (38), from their cards and model pages.

**Arena** ([dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lmarena-ai/leaderboard-dataset/tree/4217986c2fa1163d072e7a3dda637bb56cce8d79),
CC BY 4.0) is pinned at `4217986c2fa1163d072e7a3dda637bb56cce8d79`, published 2026-11-08 to
2026-11-10: 997 fresh or replacement overall observations across text, vision, document,
search, web development, image, image edit, video, image-to-video, video edit and agent.
Gemini 4 Carbon (High) leads text at 1526; Claude Fable 5.2 (xHigh) leads web development
at 1836 on 994 votes. Each identity keeps its highest reviewed effort.

**Epoch** ([archive](https://epoch.ai/data/benchmark_data.zip), CC BY 4.0), SHA-256
`4739f41b13580efd4b1012e0e402f2d1ef86ce3bd9c715a0c15dc86efd5de293`: 180 results from the
ledger-approved internal CSV members only. Across both sources, 1,044 earlier observations
were superseded by newer ones; all unrelated measurements were preserved.

Two selection rules were made explicit this time, because the first dry run would otherwise
have let a no-thinking run replace a thinking one: one row per identity is chosen first
(highest effort, then newest), and a higher-effort run replaces a lower-effort one whatever
its date, never the reverse.
