# Second pass — 14 November 2026

A completeness check on the 12 November refresh (`refresh-2026-11-12.md`), run two days
later. It found that the first pass had gaps: its maker-by-maker sweep of closed releases
never finished, and four approved independent sources were not rechecked. This pass closes
the ones that change rankings and lists the rest. It adds 4 identities, 1 benchmark and 74
net results, reaching 2,335 models, 1,312 benchmarks and 37,800 results. No scoring
formula or speed calibration was changed. Nothing was pushed or deployed.
