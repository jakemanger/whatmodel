## Outcome

- **22 sources checked.** Each one is listed in `checked`, with the quote the decision rests on.
- **9 rows proposed, all `by: "lab"` and none independent:**
  - 6 rows from the dots.tts report
  - 1 row from the HiDream-O1-Image README
  - 2 rows from the Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-Base card
- **1 comparison-only model:** `dots-tts-base`.
- **No new ledger entries or benchmarks.**
- **What changes in scoring.** I simulated this with `lib/score.ts` `toCells` on `data/` plus these rows:
  - 3 of the 63 lone results start counting: `dots-tts-soar` on `seed-tts-en-whisper-v3-wer` and `seed-tts-zh-paraformer-cer`, and `hidream-o1-image` on `dpg-bench`.
  - Two new 2-model dots.tts runs also count, on `seed-tts-en-wavlm-sim` and `seed-tts-zh-wavlm-sim`.
  - The two Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-0.6B-Base rows give their runs a second model. They still don't count, because those `-asr-unstated` tests have only 2 models in total and the scorer needs 3 (`MIN_MODELS`).
- **Check.** I imported the models and results into a scratch copy of the repo and ran `node scripts/check.ts --validate-only`. It passes.
- **Most common reasons for skipping a source:**
  1. There is no statement that the authors ran the baselines. Comparator rows carry citations and match figures printed elsewhere, so they were copied. This applied to GLM-Image, LongCat-Image, Spark-TTS, BAGEL, UniWorld-V1, Emu3, Janus, HiDream (other makers' rows), OmniGen2 and Kandinsky 2.1 (other makers' rows).
  2. The statement is mixed ("official implementations ... or as reported in their original papers") and no cell is marked as the authors' own. This applied to VoxCPM and dots.tts (baselines).
  3. The same-family comparator is not released or not catalogued: VoxCPM-Emilia, Emu3-DPO, HiDream-O1-Image-Pro, Qwen3-TTS 25Hz, the MobileWan 480p fine-tune and Kandinsky 1.0.
  4. The run is lone by design: Kandinsky 3 pairwise studies, ZONOS2 Table VII, and AMD's MXFP tables (quantisations of the same checkpoint).
  5. Values exist only in an image (Qwen-Image `bench.png`, Wan2.1 `vben_vs_sota.png`).
